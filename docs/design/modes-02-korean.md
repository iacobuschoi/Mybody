# Korean Gym / PT-Industry Vernacular for Physique Goals — Research Sweep

## 0. Method and honesty about sources

I ran ~20 Korean- and English-language web searches. **A large fraction of Korean domains are blocked by this session's egress proxy** — `namu.wiki`, `ko.wikipedia.org`, `fmkorea.com`, `brunch.co.kr`, `maily.so`, `tilnote.io`, `thewiki.kr`, `k-health.com`, `dr-allthings.com` all returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED` on WebFetch. I could not open them directly.

So I have three tiers of confidence, marked throughout:

- **[quoted]** — a verbatim Korean sentence surfaced in the search-result extraction of a named page. I did not open the page, but the sentence is attributed and specific.
- **[attested]** — I verified the term is in live use because real thread titles / article titles containing it exist on Korean lifter platforms (몬스터짐, Blind 헬스·다이어트, dcinside 헬스갤/보디빌딩 마갤, 에펨코리아). Thread titles are strong usage evidence even without opening the thread.
- **[unverified]** — my inference; reasoning stated.

I did **not** invent any citation. Where I mark something unverified, that is exactly what it means.

## 1. The glossary — what each term actually means to Korean lifters

### 1.1 The fat-loss cluster

| Term | Meaning in Korean use | Register | Really used? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **다이어트** | The default, universal Korean word for "losing weight / being on a diet." Covers everything from 절식 to a structured cut. Heavily coded toward *weight on the scale*, and culturally female-skewed. | Standard, everyone | Universally |
| **감량** | "Reduction (of weight)." Neutral, semi-clinical Sino-Korean. Used by doctors, by 대한비만학회, and by lifters as 감량기. **Also the combat-sports word for making weight.** | Standard, neutral | Yes, very |
| **커팅** | Lifter-register: stripping fat *while holding muscle*, on top of an existing muscle base. | Gym jargon (loanword) | Yes, heavily |
| **컷팅** | Misspelling of 커팅 that is arguably more common in casual writing than the correct form. | Gym jargon, casual | Yes — extremely |
| **감량기** | "The cutting block/phase," as the counterpart of 증량기. Phase-language, used by people who cycle. | Gym jargon | Yes |
| **다욧** | Internet-cute respelling of 다이어트. | Internet slang, female-skewed | Yes, but wrong register here |

**커팅 definition [quoted]** — 나무위키 「커팅」, via search extraction: *"헬스장에서 사용되는 맥락에서 **근 손실을 최소화하며 지방을 걷어내는 일체의 행위**를 커팅이라고 한다."* The same page also settles the spelling: *"한글 표기는 '커팅'이다"* (from 영어 `cutting`) — i.e. **커팅 is orthographically correct and 컷팅 is the common error.**

**커팅 ≠ 다이어트, stated explicitly by Korean sources [quoted]:**
- 건강덕후 (healduck.com, "커팅 식단으로 살 빼는 다이어트 시작가이드"): *"다이어트와 체지방 커팅은 다릅니다. 체지방 커팅은 수분, 글리코겐, 근육 등의 감소는 최소화 하고 지방만 줄이는 체중 감소를 의미합니다."*
- 건강뉴스 (ichealthnews.com): *"체지방 커팅은 체중 감량을 넘어선 과정으로 단순히 날씬해지는 것을 넘어, 보다 건강하고 탄탄한 몸을 만드는 과정입니다."* and *"커팅의 목표는 체중 감량이 아니라 체지방 감량"*.

**감량 also means "making weight" [quoted]** — 나무위키 「체중 감량」: *"체중 감량(體重減量, weight loss)은 시합을 앞두고 체중을 줄이는 훈련이다. 태권도, 유도, 합기도, 레슬링, 복싱, 종합격투기, 역도 같이 체급별로 나눠서 경기하는 격투기 종목에서 하는 단기 감량이 있습니다."* This matters: **감량 carries a "cut water and make the number" shadow meaning** in Korean that 다이어트 does not.

### 1.2 The muscle-gain cluster

| Term | Meaning | Register | Really used? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **벌크업** | Eating in meaningful surplus to add muscle, **accepting fat gain**. Strongly connoted as intermediate/advanced-only. | Gym jargon, universal | Yes, the #1 gym word after 다이어트 |
| **클린벌크** | Bulking on a small, controlled surplus with quality food. | Gym jargon | Yes |
| **더티벌크** | Bulking on anything, high calories, fast weight gain. **Negatively connoted in Korea.** | Gym jargon | Yes, as a cautionary term |
| **린매스업** | Konglish ("lean mass up"). Gaining muscle at a small surplus while holding body fat in a set band. Functionally ≈ 클린벌크, but marketed as beginner-friendly. | Konglish gym jargon | Yes, and rising fast |
| **증량** | "Increase (of weight)." **Ambiguous:** bodyweight-gain phase *or* adding load to the bar. | Standard word, ambiguous | Yes, both senses |
| **증량기** | "The bulking block." Unambiguous (the 기 suffix forces the phase reading). | Gym jargon | Yes |
| **근성장** | "Muscle growth." The outcome, not a diet phase. Massively common. | Standard/gym, universal | Yes |
| **근비대** | Hypertrophy, the technical term. | Technical/academic | Yes, by informed people |
| **살크업** | Pun: 살(fat)+벌크업. "You didn't bulk, you just got fat." Mocking. | Slang, derisive | Yes |
| **메인게이닝** | Konglish "maingaining." | Fringe | Barely — [unverified] |

**벌크업 is gatekept to non-beginners [quoted]** — 나무위키 「벌크업」, via search extraction: *"벌크업이라는 건 기존의 운동과 식단으로는 더 이상 성장이 힘든 웨이트 중급자-고급자들이 체지방의 증가를 감수하면서까지 과한 영양소의 섭취로 근비대를 이끌어내는 것을 말하는 것이지, 기초 근력도 없고 자세조차 잡히지 못한 초보자들이 따라하다가는 체지방률만 올라간다."* Also: *"운동경력이 낮은 초보자는 벌크업 따위 필요없고 규칙적으로 열심히 운동하면 된다."*

**벌크업 obligates a 커팅 afterward [quoted]** — 뷰티쁠 (beautypl.com): *"벌크업은 영양소를 필요 이상으로 섭취해 체지방과 근육을 동시에 키우는 것... **벌크업 후에는 지방을 덜어내는 커팅 과정이 필수입니다.**"* This is the single most important structural fact about Korean gym vocabulary: **벌크업 and 커팅 are understood as a paired cycle**, not as independent options.

**린매스업 definition and numbers [quoted]** — 뷰티쁠 / 얼루어 코리아 / 몬스터짐 threads:
- *"린매스업은 '군살 없는'을 뜻하는 영어 린(Lean)과 증량을 의미하는 매스 업(Mass Up)의 합성어로, 근육 합성에 필요한 최소한의 체지방만 있는 상태를 유지하면서 근육을 늘리는 것"*
- Body-fat band cited for 린매스업: **남 13~18%, 여 18~24%** (얼루어 코리아) — and a competing figure of **남 12~15%, 여 21~24%** from the 클린벌크 search cluster. These disagree; treat the band as roughly **남 12~18%**.
- Surplus size: **초보 +300~400 kcal, 중상급자 +200~300 kcal** [quoted, from the 클린벌크 search cluster]. A separate Threads coach post cites **+200~300 kcal, 단백질 2 g/kg**.
- Rate target: *"한달에 체중 1kg 증량을 목표로 잡을 수 있습니다"* [quoted, 얼루어 코리아 cluster] — ~1 kg/month, which is fast for an intermediate but matches the Korean 린매스업 marketing.

**린매스업 is Konglish.** I could not find an English-language source using "lean mass up" as a phase name (searches for it return only Korean content). The English equivalents are *lean bulk* / *clean bulk*. [unverified as a formal claim, but the search evidence is one-sided: every hit is Korean.]

**더티벌크 is discouraged in Korean coaching content [quoted]** — a Threads post (@tae__ttt) citing literature: *"더티벌크 = 지방 증가율↑ 근육 합성 효율↓ ... 클린벌크 전략은? 200~300kcal 잉여 섭취, 고단백(2g/kg)"*; another (@officiald.kr): *"결론: 클린 벌크가 가장 효율적 → 칼로리 초과 유지 + 체지방 최소화 + 근육 성장 최적화."* Treat Threads posts as *industry vernacular evidence*, not as science.

### 1.3 The simultaneous / maintenance cluster

| Term | Meaning | Register | Really used? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **상승다이어트** | **The native Korean word for body recomposition.** "Diet where the numbers go up" — muscle up, fat down. | Gym jargon, somewhat dated | Yes, genuinely attested |
| **리컴프 / 리컴포지션 / 바디 리컴포지션** | Transliterated "recomp." | English-literate niche | Barely |
| **유지** / **유지칼로리** | Maintenance / TDEE. 유지칼로리 is extremely common. | Gym jargon | Yes, 유지칼로리 especially |
| **유지어터 / 유지어트** | 유지 + 다이어터: someone maintaining after a successful diet. | Diet-community neologism, female-skewed | Yes, but not gym register |
| **유지기** | Maintenance *block*. | Gym jargon | Yes, less common than 감량기/증량기 |
| **역다이어트 / 리버스 다이어트** | Post-diet gradual calorie increase. | Semi-technical, spreading | Growing |
| **미니컷** | Short aggressive cut mid-bulk. | Essentially not Korean vernacular | **No** |

**상승다이어트 is the term you want, not 리컴프.** Evidence:
- A Korean YouTube video titled 「지방 감량 & 근육 증가 동시에 하는 방법 [상승다이어트]」 [attested].
- 몬스터짐 coach-board thread titles: 「상승다이어트 가능한가요? 식단에 관하여」, 「상승 다이어트 하는 방법」 [attested] — i.e. ordinary lifters ask coaches about it by that name.
- A Threads coach post (@100propt) titled 「지방 감량 & 근육 증가 동시에 하는 [상승 다이어트] 10가지」 [attested].
- A Blind 헬스·다이어트 thread titled 「벌크업?? 살크업?? 린매스업?? **상승다이어트**??」 [attested] — a beginner listing the four options they've heard of, and 상승다이어트 is one of them while 리컴프 is not.
- Definition [quoted]: *"상승다이어트는 근육량 증가와 체지방, 체중 감소를 동시에 하는 다이어트입니다. 외국에서는 'BODY RECOMPOSITION'으로 불리며 직역하면 신체 재구성입니다."*

By contrast, searching `"리컴프" 헬스 커뮤니티 한국` returned **almost no Korean community usage** — the top hits were a Cambridge dictionary page, a Discord server for 림버스컴퍼니, and a supplement product called "리콤프 RX." The only genuine hit was one newsletter post (maily.so 「#3 벌크, 다이어트, 리컴프 어떤 것 부터?」). **리컴프 is an English-reader's word in Korea, not a gym-floor word.**

**미니컷 is not Korean vernacular.** Searching `미니컷 헬스 증량 중간 감량` in Korean returned a plastic-surgery clinic page (미니플러스 리프팅) and then **only English-language sources** (The Bodybuilding Dietitians, Built With Science, GainFrame, Elevated Coaching Systems — all giving 2–6 weeks, 500–750 kcal deficit, 2.4–2.6 g/kg protein). Korean lifters who do this call it 단기 커팅 — attested by the 몬스터짐 thread title 「**단기 커팅**중인데 잠깐 봐주시겠어요?ㅠㅠ」 [attested]. **If you build a mini-cut, label it 단기 커팅, not 미니컷.**

**유지어터** [quoted] — *"유지어트는 유지+다이어트의 합성으로 다이어트 이후 빠진 체중을 유지하기 위한 식습관 및 생활습관"*; *"유지어터란 다이어트 이후 감량된 몸무게를 유지하는 사람"*. It appeared in mainstream "다이어트 신조어" roundups (헤럴드 2019, 쿠키뉴스 2019, 시사오늘) alongside 방탄커피 and 치팅데이 — which places it as **2019-era diet-trend slang, not lifter slang.** A 22-year-old male lifter will know it but will not identify with it.

**역다이어트 / 리버스 다이어팅** [quoted] — GQ Korea (2025-04) ran 「많이 먹을수록 몸이 좋아지는 의외의 방법, 리버스 다이어트」; Nutrola's Korean blog gives the mechanic: *"1,500칼로리에서 2,400칼로리로 갑자기 돌아가는 대신, 매주 50에서 150칼로리씩 체계적으로 추가"* and the goal *"체중 증가 없이 감량한 체중을 유지하고, 신진대사를 회복하는 것."* The concept is arriving in Korean mainstream media but is **not yet gym-floor vocabulary**.

### 1.4 Training-goal terms (not diet phases)

- **근비대** — hypertrophy. [quoted, ko.wikipedia 근비대]: *"근세포 크기 성장을 통해 골격근의 크기가 비대하거나 증가하는 것."*
- **근력 향상 / 스트렝스** — strength. [quoted, 나무위키 스트렝스 트레이닝]: *"보디빌딩식 운동이 근육의 비대를 위주로 운동하면서 부가적으로 근력과 근지구력이 따라온다면, 스트렝스 트레이닝은 근력을 위주로 운동하면서 부가적으로 근비대가 오는 것입니다."*
- **근지구력** — muscular endurance.
- **근성장** is the colloquial umbrella that a normal lifter uses where a coach would say 근비대.

**Implication for your mode names:** 근비대 is correct but reads as a textbook. **근성장 is the word a real person uses.** The owner's guess of "근성장모드" is the *best* of their four.

### 1.5 The InBody vocabulary your UI already inherits

Because the app ingests an InBody sheet, these are non-negotiable label choices [quoted, from KB손해보험 인사이트 / 이로움돌봄 / 공여사들 InBody explainers]:

- **골격근량 (SMM, Skeletal Muscle Mass)** — *"SMM은 Skeletal Muscle Mass의 약자로, 골격근량"*
- **체지방량 (BFM, Body Fat Mass)** — *"BFM은 Body Fat Mass의 약자로, 체지방량"*
- **체지방률 (PBF)** — *"체중에서 체지방량이 차지하는 비율... 표준적인 남성의 체지방률은 15%, 여성은 25%"*
- **D자형 체형** — *"체중/골격근량/체지방량의 그래프바의 끝을 선으로 이었을 때, D자형은 골격근량이 상대적으로 많은 건강한 체형으로, 가장 이상적인 체형"*

**D자형 is a culturally loaded, instantly-recognized Korean goal-state.** Nearly every Korean who has stood on an InBody knows whether their chart is D자, I자, or C자. This is a *huge* free UX win: you can express "what this mode is aiming at" as a shape change on the InBody bar chart, in a language the owner already speaks.

### 1.6 The event-driven cluster

- **바디프로필 / 바프** — see §3. Short form **바프** is standard [quoted, 한국경제 튜브뉴스: *"'바프'는 '바디프로필 사진'의 줄임말"*].
- **대회 준비 / 시합 준비 / 프렙** — bodybuilding contest prep. 몬스터짐 runs a column series literally titled 「[시합준비] 보디빌딩 시합을 준비하는 영양」 [attested]. **시합 준비** is the native phrase; **프렙** is the loanword used by the competitive crowd.
- **비시즌 / 오프시즌** — the non-competition phase, i.e. the bulk.
- **치팅데이** — universally known [quoted, Threads 「요즘 헬스어 정리」: *"치팅데이 = 식단을 잠시 쉬는 날"*]. Also in 나무위키 as its own article.
- **폭식** — binge. Clinical/negative. Not a feature name.

## 2. CRITICAL: is 감량 the same as 커팅?

### Verdict: **No. They are different words for different situations, and your app should have both — but as two entry states of one engine direction, not as two unrelated modes.**

**The evidence that Korean speakers feel them as different:**

1. Korean explainer sites open by *denying* the equivalence, which you only do when readers assume it. 건강덕후: *"다이어트와 체지방 커팅은 다릅니다."* 건강뉴스: *"체지방 커팅은 체중 감량을 넘어선 과정."*
2. The distinction is **actively argued about** in Korean fitness media. A Threads post (@ji.hoox) is built entirely around the question 「**보디빌더 커팅은 일반인 다이어트와 다른가?**」 and quotes a commenter insisting *"빌더가 커팅하는 개념과 일반인의 다이어트는 전혀 달라요."* A term that was a pure synonym would not generate that debate.
3. 나무위키's 커팅 definition contains a constraint that 감량's does not: *"**근 손실을 최소화하며** 지방을 걷어내는."* 감량 has no such clause — 나무위키's 체중 감량 article is about **making weight in combat sports**, including water cuts.
4. Search-hit asymmetry by platform. On 몬스터짐 (serious lifters + coaches) the thread titles are 「커팅 질문」, 「커팅시 운동분할」, 「커팅 시 근손실 비율이 어느정도 인가요?」, 「단기 커팅중인데...」, 「컷팅에 대해서 알고싶습니다」 [attested]. On general-health and women's-media sites the word is 다이어트/감량. **The word choice tracks the speaker's identity, not just the goal.**

**The real semantic difference, stated precisely:**

| | 감량 (or 다이어트) | 커팅 |
|---|---|---|
| Presupposes | Nothing. Anyone can 감량. | **You already have a muscle base worth revealing.** |
| Target variable | 체중 (the scale number) | 체지방률 / definition |
| Muscle | A thing to try not to lose | **The entire point of the exercise** |
| Typical entry PBF (남) | > 20%, often 25%+ | 13–20% |
| Typical duration | Long, 12–24+ weeks | Short, 6–12 weeks |
| What comes before | Nothing / life | A 벌크업 or 린매스업 |
| What comes after | 유지 (or, sadly, 요요) | 증량기, or a photoshoot |
| Who says it | Everyone | Lifters |

There is a Korean gym-floor corollary that makes this concrete [quoted, from the kormedi.com / 나무위키 cluster]: *"체지방이 많고 허리둘레가 부담스럽다면 커팅이 먼저일 수 있다. 그러나 초보자는 처음부터 과한 다이어트보다 가벼운 칼로리 적자와 근력 운동을 함께 하는 편이 좋다."* — i.e. even Korean writers who use "커팅" for a fat beginner immediately walk it back toward a gentler approach, because the aggressive 커팅 protocol is wrong for that person.

### Concrete recommendation for the product

**Ship both names, but make them one engine direction with a PBF-gated split, and say the gate out loud in the UI.**

- **감량 모드** — entry: 남 PBF ≥ 20% / 여 PBF ≥ 30%, *or* the user's ΔSMM target is ≈ 0 and ΔBFM is large. Wider `a` range (the engine can afford a real deficit here because there's plenty of BFM to feed the 31 × BFM kcal/day mobilization ceiling). Long duration. Exit at a PBF number.
- **커팅 모드** — entry: 남 PBF 13–20% / 여 18–28%, **and** SMM already at or above the user's own recent peak. Narrow, capped `a` (this is the mode that must *refuse* to go past ~15% of TDEE, because the engine's own muscle-retention factor collapses from 0.7 → 0.30 for a novice and 0.35 → 0.05 for an intermediate once the deficit exceeds 15% of TDEE). Short duration. Exit at a look/PBF, then hand off to 유지 or 증량.

**Do not merge them,** because the two need genuinely different `a` ceilings and different refusals, and because a Korean user with 28% PBF who is told "커팅 모드" will correctly feel the app doesn't understand him. And do not pretend they are unrelated — the UI line should be roughly:

> **감량** — 체중을 먼저 줄이는 단계. 근육은 지키는 게 목표.
> **커팅** — 이미 근육이 있고, 체지방만 걷어내서 선명하게 만드는 단계.

That one pair of sentences resolves the ambiguity the owner sensed.

## 3. 벌크업 vs 증량 vs 린매스업

### Verdict: **three different words, only one of which should be a mode name — and it should be 린매스업.**

- **증량** is **ambiguous and therefore disqualified as a mode name.** In Korean gym speech it means *both* "gaining bodyweight" and "adding load to the bar." Evidence [attested]: real Q&A titles include 「헬스에서 **무게 증량**하는 시점을 어떻게 잡나요?」 and 「헬스할때 **무게증량**은 언제시작하면 되나요?」 — alongside 「증량기」 used for the bulk phase. The suffixed form **증량기** disambiguates, but a mode button reading "증량" will be misread by some fraction of users as a strength-progression setting. Use it only as the *phase* word inside prose ("증량기 12주 → 감량기 8주"), never as the button.
- **벌크업** is unambiguous and universally understood — but it carries a **specific commitment to accepting fat gain**, and Korean sources gatekeep it to intermediates. If you label your muscle-gain mode 벌크업, you are promising the user fat gain, and every Korean article they read will tell them they shouldn't be doing it. It is also inseparable from its obligatory sequel: *"벌크업 후에는 지방을 덜어내는 커팅 과정이 필수"*.
- **린매스업** is the Korean fitness industry's **currently ascendant** answer to exactly that problem, and it has crossed over into mainstream women's and lifestyle media: 뷰티쁠 「벌크업 대신 린매스업」, 얼루어 코리아 「다이어트 대신 린매스업하세요」, plus 몬스터짐 coach threads 「린매스업 식단 질문드립니다」 and 「한달간 린메스업을 해본 결과.. 체지방이 잘 안빠지네요」 [attested — note the misspelling 린메스업, which is itself evidence of casual spoken use].

**Practical mapping for the engine:**

- 린매스업 ≈ `a` in the *low surplus* band: +200~400 kcal, ~+0.25–0.5%BW/month, protein 2 g/kg, PBF held inside 남 12~18%. This is a **hard fit** for your engine's `surplus ×1.0` situational factor with a small `a`.
- 벌크업 = the same direction with a larger `a` and an explicit "expect 체지방률 +X%p" disclosure. **I recommend offering it only as an "aggressive" setting *inside* 린매스업 (i.e. your 상 speed), not as its own mode.** That collapses two of the owner's guesses into one and matches how Korean content actually frames the choice ("벌크업 **대신** 린매스업").
- 더티벌크 — **never** offer. Every Korean source I found treats it as the failure mode, and 살크업 exists purely to mock it.

One caveat worth flagging to the owner: the Korean 린매스업 marketing target of **1 kg/month of bodyweight gain** is *above* what your engine will allow for anyone past novice (intermediate ceiling 0.75 %BW/month of FFM ≈ 0.53 kg/month at 70 kg). **Your engine will therefore produce slower, more honest numbers than the Korean articles the owner has read.** Say so in the UI, or the owner will think the app is broken.

## 4. 바디프로필 — the Korean deadline phenomenon

This is, in my judgment, **the single most important Korean-specific finding in this sweep**, and it does not fit the four modes the owner proposed.

### Scale

[quoted] 엠브레인 트렌드모니터 survey, 전국 19~59세 성인 1,000명 (reported by 매드타임스 / 오픈애즈):
- 바디프로필 유행 체감도: **20대 60.4%**, 30대 49.2%, 40대 29.2%, 50대 15.6%
- "주변에서 촬영하는 경우가 많다": 20대 58.4%, 30대 55.6%
- 촬영 의향 있음: 전체 10명 중 4명; **20대 47.2%**, 30대 46.0%

For a 22-year-old Korean male, this is not a niche. **Roughly half his cohort intends to shoot one.**

### How Korean coaches structure a prep

[quoted, from the 바디프로필 준비 search cluster — 가지랩, 근성장연구소, 씨즈더데이 스튜디오 guides]:
- **Baseline recommendation: 최소 3개월 / 12주.** *"바디프로필 촬영을 할 경우 적어도 3개월인 12주 정도의 준비 기간을 가지는 것이 권장됩니다."*
- **If starting fat: 16~20주.** *"체지방이 많은 경우는 16주에서 20주 정도 준비 기간을 가져야 합니다."*
- **Explicit anti-crash guidance exists in the better guides:** *"같은 식단으로 3개월 내내 유지하지 않고, **극단적인 식단을 2주 이상 끌지 않습니다.**"*
- PT is assumed: *"준비 기간 동안 PT를 병행하는 것이 좋으며, 시작 전 트레이너와 바디프로필의 목표와 운동 계획에 대해 충분히 상의하고 시작해야 합니다."*

**The final week (D-7) is a separate, non-nutritional protocol** [quoted, from a 바디프로필 D-7 blog]:
- **수분조절 / 단수** — *"다량의 수분을 섭취하다가 섭취량을 점차 줄여 단수하는 과정을 통해 근육이 펌핑되고 선명해 보이도록"*; typical amateur schedule *"촬영 6일 전부터 3일간 2L 이상씩 섭취하고 남은 3일간은 500ml, 300ml, 단수"*.
- **탄수로딩** and **밴딩** on the day.
- Low-sodium day-before meals; no training the day before.

**This last week is outside your engine's physics entirely** (it is water and glycogen, not SMM/BFM) and it is the part with real medical risk. **Do not model it. Do not generate it. Consider explicitly refusing to.**

### Known harms — well documented in Korean mainstream press

[quoted, from 경향신문/헬스경향, 여성경제신문, 시사저널, 헬스조선 헬스케어N, 한국경제 튜브뉴스]:
- Reported primary symptoms: **거식증, 폭식증, 요요현상, 생리중단.** *"주증상은 거식증이나 폭식증, 요요현상, 생리중단 등입니다."*
- Mechanism: *"식습관에 엄격한 기준이 지속되면 식사행위 자체에 불안을 느끼고 음식을 거부하는 거식증, 보상심리로 인한 폭식증이 발생합니다."*
- Rebound: *"촬영일이 다가올수록 초절식에 가까운 식단을 유지해야 하는데, 촬영이 끝난 후 정상 식단을 회복했을 때 체중 증가 속도도 빠릅니다."*
- A psychiatrist is quoted (reported as 삼성서울병원 정신건강의학과 안지현 교수 — **name and affiliation via search extraction only, I could not open the article, treat as [quoted-but-unconfirmed]**): *"바디프로필 촬영 자체가 목표가 되면서 정해진 기간에 극단적 식단관리와 운동으로 각종 문제가 발생하는 것."*
- Generalized risk statement: *"바디프로필이나 피트니스 대회처럼 극단적인 다이어트가 필요한 상황과 치팅 경험이 반복되면 식이장애로 이어질 수 있습니다."*
- 몬스터짐 has a coach-board thread titled 「**바디프로필 후 계속되는 폭식.. 우울증 오는 것 같아요**」 [attested] — this is not a media panic; the lifters themselves post about it.
- One interviewee's claim of shoot-day body fat **"남자는 5%, 여자는 10%"** appeared in 한국경제 튜브뉴스 [quoted, but this is a layperson's self-report; 남 5% is below the essential-fat line and I would treat it as aspirational/exaggerated — mark [unverified] as a norm].

### Product recommendation

**바디프로필 is not a fifth mode. It is a hard deadline flag on 커팅, plus a set of refusals.**

Concretely, in your engine's terms:
- It is the **only** input that legitimately inverts the app's logic: instead of "here is when you'll get there at 상/중/하," it is "the date is fixed, what's reachable?" That is a different *solve*, not a different mode — you're solving for reachable Δ given fixed weeks, rather than for weeks given fixed Δ.
- It should **cap `a`**, not raise it, and it should surface a refusal when the requested ΔBFM in the available weeks exceeds either the 31 × BFM kcal/day ceiling or the calorie floor. The single most valuable thing this app can do for a Korean 20-something is show them, in numbers, that their 8-week 바프 goal is arithmetically impossible — *before* they crash-diet toward it.
- It should hard-refuse to generate 단수/수분조절/탄수로딩 and say why.
- It should ship a **post-shoot plan** by default (역다이어트: +50~150 kcal/week per the Nutrola figure, into 유지). Korean press documents the rebound; an app that ends at the shutter is participating in the harm.

## 6. Terms to AVOID

### 6.1 Avoid for harm / official censure

**헬린이 — do not use, under any circumstances.**

The 국가인권위원회 (National Human Rights Commission of Korea) formally issued an **의견표명** that `~린이` compounds are 아동 비하 표현, recommending 문화체육관광부 educate against their use in public documents and asking 방송통신심의위원회 to strengthen monitoring [quoted, reported 2022-05 by SBS 뉴스딱, YTN, MBC, 이데일리, 한국일보, 더나은미래]. The reasoning quoted: *"'~린이'라는 표현을 사용하는 것은 아동이 미숙하고 불완전한 존재라는 인식에 기반한 것으로, 아동에 대한 부정적인 고정관념을 조장할 수 있다."*

It remains extremely common on 에펨코리아 and dcinside. It is nonetheless a **documented, officially-flagged slur pattern** and has no place in a shipped UI. Use **초보 / 입문** for the novice training-age tier.

**폭식 / 치팅데이 as feature names — avoid.** 치팅데이 is harmless as a word people know, but naming an app feature after it, in a product whose Korean user base has a documented 바디프로필-driven binge problem (*"극단적인 다이어트가 필요한 상황과 치팅 경험이 반복되면 식이장애로 이어질 수 있습니다"*), is a bad idea. If you need the concept, call it **리피드(refeed)** or **계획된 고탄수일** and tie it to a physiological rationale, not to permission-to-binge framing.

**단수 / 수분조절 — do not implement, do not name.** It is the genuinely dangerous part of 바프 prep, it is pure water/glycogen (invisible to a {SMM, BFM} state model), and generating it would be the app making a medical recommendation it cannot support.

**초절식 / 극단 다이어트 framing — avoid** even as marketing copy for the 상 speed. The press coverage uses 초절식 as the villain word.

### 6.2 Avoid for register / tone

- **헬창** — [quoted, 나무위키 헬창] *"헬창 자체는 욕이지만 너무나도 자주 쓰이는 탓에 강도가 많이 약해졌으며, 헬갤러 스스로도 자학 개그의 형태로 이 단어를 사용합니다."* Self-deprecating in-group slang. Fine between friends; an app calling the user 헬창 is presumptuous.
- **다욧** — [quoted, wordrow.kr] *"주로 인터넷상에서 '다이어트'라는 뜻으로 쓰는 말."* Cutesy, female-community-coded, ~2015 vintage. Wrong register for a 22-year-old male lifter and it drags in diet-culture connotations.
- **살크업** — a mockery term ("you got fat, not big"). Never label a user's outcome this, no matter how accurate.
- **더티벌크** — don't offer it as a choice. Korean coaching content is unanimously against it; offering it as a button implies endorsement.
- **유지어터** — not wrong, but it's 2019 diet-trend slang from the same news cycle as 방탄커피. Use **유지** or **유지 단계** for the maintenance mode; a male lifter will not call himself a 유지어터.

### 6.3 Avoid for ambiguity

- **증량** as a button label — reads as "add weight to the bar" to a meaningful fraction of users (§3). Use 증량기 in prose only.
- **감량** *alone*, with no subtitle, if you also ship 커팅 — the owner's instinct that these collide is correct, and the fix is the one-line subtitle, not deleting one.
- **컷팅** as the *spelling* — use **커팅**. 나무위키 is explicit that 커팅 is correct. But **accept 컷팅 in search/autocomplete**, because it is what people type.
- **리컴프** as a primary label — near-zero recognition (§5).
- **미니컷** — use **단기 커팅**.

## 8. One-paragraph answer to the owner's actual question

감량과 커팅은 한국 헬스장에서 같은 말이 아니다 — 감량은 "몸무게를 줄인다"는 일반 단어고, 커팅은 "**이미 근육이 있는 사람이** 근손실 없이 체지방만 걷어낸다"는 리프터 전용 단어다. 그래서 두 개 다 남기되, 체지방률로 자동 분기시키고 한 줄 설명을 붙이면 된다. 근성장모드는 네 개 중 제일 잘 고른 이름이고, 리컴프모드만 이름을 바꿔야 한다 — 한국 헬스 커뮤니티에서 "리컴프"는 거의 안 쓰는 말이라서, **동시 개선** 또는 **상승다이어트**가 맞다. 그리고 네 개로는 부족하다: **유지** 모드가 하나 더 있어야 하고, **바디프로필**은 모드가 아니라 "커팅 + 마감일" 플래그로 붙여야 한다 — 20대 절반이 찍겠다고 하는 문화 현상인데, 후유증(폭식, 요요, 생리중단)도 언론에 충분히 기록되어 있어서 앱이 "그 날짜엔 수학적으로 불가능합니다"라고 말해줄 수 있는 게 이 앱의 제일 큰 가치다.
